### Project Title
**SnapClip — Smart Snippet & Clipboard Vault**

### Tagline
**Your snippets, templates, and essential links — always ready in a snap.**

---

### Elevator Pitch / Description
SnapClip solves the daily friction of re-typing standard responses, searching for lost links, and switching between apps to copy snippets. Designed with speed, elegance, and power in mind, SnapClip gives power users, creators, and developers one unified vault for all their text, code snippets, web links, and templates.

---

### What It Does
- **1-Tap Clipboard Copying**: Copies full templates, code blocks, or URLs with haptic confirmation.
- **Smart Categorization**: Custom emoji folders and tag filtering for zero-friction organization.
- **Instant Search**: Real-time multi-attribute search across snippet titles, content, and `#tags`.
- **Integrated Browser Launcher**: Open saved URLs directly from your snippets in one tap.
- **RevenueCat Paywall & Freemium Structure**: Upgrades users to SnapClip Pro with an annual free trial, unlimited vaults, and pro utilities.

---

### How We Built It
- **Flutter & Dart**: Cross-platform architecture ensuring pixel-perfect Material 3 design across Android and Galaxy Store.
- **RevenueCat SDK (`purchases_flutter`)**: Implemented dynamic offerings, entitlements validation, and in-app subscription management with automated sandbox restoration.
- **Riverpod 2.0**: Robust, reactive state management powering snippet search, filtering, and live subscription status.
- **Hive NoSQL**: Ultra-fast local database guaranteeing instant offline read/write access.
